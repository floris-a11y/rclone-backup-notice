Privacy notice

Updated 26 October 2026.

This notice describes a private, single-operator rclone configuration. It is not a public service.

Google Drive access

The operator authorizes access to their own Drive account to list, upload, download, verify and manage backup files. Files are transferred between Google Drive and the operator’s own computers.

Credentials and data handling

OAuth credentials are stored locally on the operator’s computers. Google processes authentication and stores the Drive files under its own policies. This configuration does not run an external data-collection server, advertising or analytics.

The configuration does not sell Google user data or send it to advertising services. Upstream rclone is an independent open-source project.

Control

The operator can revoke the application’s access through their Google Account and can remove locally stored credentials. Google Drive file retention is controlled by the operator’s backup and recovery rules.
